Welcome to the **Enterprise Homelab** documentation. This environment started as a **CCNA practice lab** and is evolving into a real network lab for NetDevOps experiments and Linux systems administration.

## Roadmap

The project is organized into two phases:

1. **[Phase 1 — CCNA Lab & Emulation (Completed)](/enterprise-homelab/phase-1-ccna-lab/)** — the practice lab used to earn the CCNA: HP t740 compute node, Catalyst 3560-CX, 2960 access, Proxmox VE, and EVE-NG emulation.
2. **[Phase 2 — Infrastructure Expansion (Roadmap)](/enterprise-homelab/phase-2-infrastructure/)** — **OPNsense router** on the HP t740 (DPI, AdGuard, firewall, GL.iNet MT3000 as AP), and everything managed **as code** with Ansible.
