<Aside type="note" title="Status: Completed — Sandbox, Not Production">
This phase was a **practice lab (sandbox) built to prepare for the Cisco CCNA**. It was never a production network: configurations were temporary and recreated per lab.
</Aside>

Phase 1 documents the lab I used to earn the CCNA. It was a sandbox for **practicing** Cisco switching, routing, and emulation topics — physical Cisco switches for real Layer 2/3 behavior, Proxmox virtualization, and EVE-NG for virtual nodes. None of it was intended to serve real end devices.

## What It Was Used For

* **Switching practice:** VLANs, 802.1Q trunking, inter-VLAN routing (SVIs), STP, and LACP scenarios.
* **Routing practice:** OSPF and routing fundamentals in EVE-NG, bridged to the physical switches.
* **Real hardware feel:** a Catalyst 3560-CX (L3) and a Catalyst 2960 (L2) to practice on real IOS instead of emulation alone.
* **Incident practice:** real Layer 1/2/3 failures during build-out, documented in [Troubleshooting](/enterprise-homelab/phase-1-ccna-lab/5-troubleshooting/catalyst-autonegotiation/).
