## OSPF Lab

Single-area OSPF practice in EVE-NG: building adjacencies, DR/BDR election, and path selection across a multi-router topology bridged to the physical switches.

![OSPF lab topology](/OSPF-topology-lab.png)

*OSPF topology used for adjacency, DR/BDR, and path-selection practice.*




## SNMP Server Lab

SNMP monitoring of the physical Catalyst 2960 and a virtual IOSv router in EVE-NG using the iReasoning MIB Browser, with agents isolated in the management VLAN (102). SNMP v2c and v3 configured.

## Switching / VLAN Topology

Multi-switch Layer 2 topology for VLAN, trunking, and inter-VLAN routing practice between the Catalyst 3560-CX and 2960.

<ThemeImage lightSrc="/vlan-lab-topology-light.svg" darkSrc="/vlan-lab-topology-dark.svg" alt="VLAN lab topology" />

*VLAN/trunk topology across the physical switches.*

The four lab PCs are **VMs on the HP t740**, each mapped to a physical network card bridged to the 3560-CX, so virtual hosts reach the physical switches over real cabling.
