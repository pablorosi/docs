The long-term goal is to manage the homelab **as code** with **Ansible**: provisioning, configuring, and keeping in sync everything that runs in Phase 2 (router, services, workloads).

## Planned Scope

* Inventory of the homelab devices and their roles.
* Playbooks to configure the OPNsense router and the services on top of it.
* One place to re-create or re-provision the whole lab from scratch.

*Details will be added here as the project takes shape.*
